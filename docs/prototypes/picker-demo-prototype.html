What is being prototyped

Hover or drag any handle or leg on the hexagon and a tooltip appears beside every leg it drives: red below, green north-east, blue north-west. Dragging lights each leg and joint it drives, itself included. While a control is held, every slider and leg whose own value changed lights up and stays lit until you let go, then fades, the same crossfade the app uses for its brightness-limit hexagon. The ? button plays the demo.
