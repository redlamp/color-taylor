Handle study

Color Taylor's picked-point marker is already the same construction as Figma's - a colour-filled circle with a white ring and a shadow. The question is whether to raise it to Figma's finish and use it in more places. Everything below is the real geometry, not a mockup.

1. The hard cases

A handle has to stay visible when its own colour matches the field behind it. These are the combinations that break weak handles: white on white, black on black, and mid-tones on mid-tones.

4. What not to change

The hue and brightness pills carry their value at the point of interaction. Figma cannot do this - its numbers live in a field row at the bottom of the panel. Replacing these with anonymous rings would trade information for familiarity.

Suggested rule: ring where there is no number, pill where there is. That leaves one marker vocabulary for positions and one for values, instead of today's four.
