The RGB stems in circle space

The hexagon is a linear projection of the RGB cube, which is the only reason three straight legs at 120° add up to the colour. The circle is not. Drag Shape to 0 and watch what a straight line has to become.

Why they bendHexagon to circle is a radial stretch of R / hexEdge(θ) — exactly 1 at the vertices, 1.155 at the edge midpoints. Angle-dependent, so not affine, so the sum of the warped vectors is not the warp of the sum. Warping the joints and letting the legs curve is the only option that keeps each leg meaning “add this much of this channel”.
The first leg never bendsIt runs from the centre along a channel axis, and all three axes point at vertices, where the stretch is exactly 1. So the chain starts honest and accumulates distortion. The chords are the cheap version: visually close, but the points along one do not correspond to intermediate channel values, so hovering or dragging a leg would lie.
