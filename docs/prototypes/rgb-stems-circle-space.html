The RGB stems in circle space

The hexagon is a linear projection of the RGB cube, which is the only reason three straight legs at 120° add up to the colour. The circle is not. Drag Shape to 0 and watch what a straight line has to become.

Why they bendHexagon to circle is a radial stretch of R / hexEdge(θ) — exactly 1 at the vertices, 1.155 at the edge midpoints. Angle-dependent, so not affine, so the sum of the warped vectors is not the warp of the sum. Warping the joints and letting the legs curve is the only option that keeps each leg meaning “add this much of this channel”.
The first leg never bendsIt runs from the centre along a channel axis, and all three axes point at vertices, where the stretch is exactly 1. So the chain starts honest and accumulates distortion. The chords are the cheap version: visually close, but the points along one do not correspond to intermediate channel values, so hovering or dragging a leg would lie.
The RGB sliders drive the chain directlyEach leg is its channel's value, so dragging Red lengthens the red leg and nothing else moves — in the hexagon. In the circle the later joints shift anyway, because they sit at angles whose stretch changed.
Why HSB stays canonical hereSame reason as ColorPicker.tsx: hsbToRgb(rgbToHsb(rgb)) is lossy at low saturation and brightness, so typing an exact R and then dragging would lose it. The exact RGB is stashed in an override and the HSB sliders clear it.
Oklch is a different kind of axisIts lightness is perceptual, so holding L and turning the hue keeps the colour looking equally bright — which HSB cannot do, because B means “how far to this hue's ceiling” and the ceilings differ. Its hue zero is not red: red sits at 29°, blue at 264°, so the two hue sliders never agree and never should.
Most Oklch triples are not sRGB coloursChroma is absolute, and at any lightness the reachable chroma depends on hue — cyan tops out at 0.155, magenta at 0.322. Out-of-gamut values are mapped by reducing chroma at fixed L and H, the way CSS Color 4 does, and the group says so. The sliders hold what you set, not what survived; the field shows what survived.
