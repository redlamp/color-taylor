Inside the RGB cube

Every colour your screen can show is a corner, an edge or an interior point of this solid. Hue, saturation and brightness are not extra ideas layered on top — each one is a direction or a distance in here. Turn it, then cut it open.

The axis everything turns aroundBlack and white are opposite corners, and the line between them is the neutral axis — every grey lives on it. Stand it upright and brightness is height. Look straight down it and the outline is a regular hexagon, which is the shape of the picker.
Hue is an angle, saturation is a distanceAround that axis, direction is hue — red at 0°, green at 120°, blue at 240°, which is where DIRS comes from. Distance out from the axis is saturation. The three channel faces sit at the same elevation, 120° apart.
