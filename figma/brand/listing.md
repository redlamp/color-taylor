## Name

    Color Taylor

## Tagline

    See how RGB, HSB and HSL describe the same color.

## Description

> **Positioning note, not listing text.** Figma's review guidelines reject plugins that "recreate core Figma functionality", and Figma ships a color picker. The defence is that this is a *color model* tool - one hexagon showing how three notations describe the same color, with a swatch library Figma's picker has no equivalent for. That has to be the first sentence and the first screenshot, not a detail further down. Everything below is written to that.

    Color Taylor is a color hexagon: red, yellow, green, cyan, blue and
    magenta at the corners, white in the middle. Every color sits somewhere
    inside it, and dragging one vector at a time shows you exactly how RGB,
    HSB and HSL each describe the position you just moved to.

    It paints as you go. Select a layer, pick a color, and the fill or stroke
    updates live - no Apply button.

    WHAT IT ADDS

    - One hexagon, three notations. Drag a channel and watch the other two
      follow. The relationship between the color models is the point.
    - Sliders for RGB, HSB, HSL and alpha, shown together or separately.
      Each track can render its channel in the context of your current color
      or on its own.
    - A swatch library that persists. Twelve saved slots that grow to
      thirty-six as you fill them, drag to rearrange, and sort by hue,
      saturation, brightness or alpha. Alpha is part of a swatch, so the same
      color at two opacities is two swatches.
    - Recent colors tracked automatically. Drag one into a saved slot to keep
      it.
    - Fill, stroke, or neither - "None" reads the selection without writing
      to it.

    Each drag is its own undo step.

    PRIVACY

    Nothing leaves the plugin. There is no network access at all, declared in
    the manifest. Saved and recent colors live in Figma's clientStorage on
    your own machine.
