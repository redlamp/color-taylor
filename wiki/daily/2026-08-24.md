# 2026-08-24

Long session on the picker's colour model. Shipped [PR #73](https://github.com/redlamp/color-taylor/pull/73);
[PR #74](https://github.com/redlamp/color-taylor/pull/74) open.

## Shipped

- **`strict: true`** — closes #16. 77 errors, and not where the issue predicted: 28 of them came
  from 12 untyped `useRef(null)`, which infers `null`, narrows to `never`, and silently turns off
  checking on `.current`. Those refs were the drag machinery.
- **Radius is chroma.** The handle sat at r² instead of r, so at B=30 a click at half the radius
  landed the handle at a quarter of it. Three things disagreed about what radius meant — the canvas,
  the vector chain, and the pointer. The cube cross-section wins; see [[hexagon-is-the-cube-down-its-diagonal]].
  The field now dims beyond the reachable hexagon rather than painting the full one.
- **HSL parity**, then a run of edge cases the user found by using it: hue dying at grey, at black
  and at white; the saturation bar writing HSB's S while labelled HSL's; L inverting past the rim
  because `r` was unclamped; the stepper stuck at `#2B6FD6`.
- **A saturation bar** under the hexagon, and drag/tween highlights on both axes.
- **Mobile**: plugin announcement moved into the settings sheet, header on one row.
