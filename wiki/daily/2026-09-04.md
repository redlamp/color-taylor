## The stale dev server, again, and what it had been hiding

The `:5173` dev server another session left running had stopped picking up changes **entirely**, not
just CSS: it did not know `AboutPanel` existed. Playwright reuses whatever is already on that port,
so the whole suite had been passing against an app that no longer existed - for an unknown number of
runs.

`playwright.config.ts` takes `BASE_URL` now, and starts no server of its own when it is set. Pointed
at a `vite preview` of a fresh build, three real failures appeared immediately, all of them genuine:
the welcome panel's modal backdrop eats the first click of any test that meets it, the reset tests
had to account for it, and one locator was matching the settings sheet because that is a dialog too.

Every context now starts having seen the welcome, seeded from `tests/storage-state.json`;
`tests/about-panel.spec.ts` clears it and tests the real first-run behaviour. Root `CLAUDE.md` has
the symptom written down: a spec for something you just added failing with "element(s) not found"
while the page is plainly correct in a browser.

83 e2e, 91 unit. Eleventh localStorage key, `color-taylor-about-seen`, wired to reset-all.

The header's gear became a menu icon, and About moved from the foot of the sheet to the top of it.
Both follow from what the sheet is now: not preferences but everywhere that is not the picker, with
the way in at the top and the reset at the bottom. The audio row inside the Audio section reads
"Enable audio" rather than "Audio", which it was until it had a heading of its own to sit under.

The welcome panel took the demo caption's hairline, and the class stopped being called `demo-panel`
