## Also planned

- [[plan-intro-two-paths]] (#79, #80): a fork after the opening — the Macintosh history or
  straight to the colour models — and a period frame for the history path.
- The fork's enabling change is **routing by slide id rather than index**. `#/intro/7` means
  different slides on different paths; `#/intro/12-hexagon` does not. It also fixes the deep-link
  bug below on the way past.
- The frame cannot just change `PANEL_W`. That constant is 726 *because it matches the picker's
  panel width*, which is what lets `15-app` scale the real app into the panel's footprint. So the
  frame is a treatment over the history slides, not a change to the shared panel.
- **Reversed the narration workflow** in [[plan-narrated-intro]] and #78. It said script → record →
  captions, on the reasoning that writing it last means writing it twice. That assumed writing was
  the cheap part; it is not. Talk against the deck in one pass, transcribe, tidy, ship the
  transcript as captions. A read script would cost the delivery, which is the reason to record at
  all.
