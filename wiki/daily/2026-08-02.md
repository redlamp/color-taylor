## West-edge resize, third attempt

Dragging the west edge means resizing *and* repositioning, and it broke three separate ways - twice flinging the panel off-screen. The cause: `figma.ui.reposition` and `figma.ui.getPosition` are not documented as sharing a coordinate space (`getPosition` returns `{windowSpace, canvasSpace}`; `reposition`'s docs say nothing at all), so every "read here, write there" version was a guess.

Fix was to stop guessing and **prove it at runtime**: nudge the window a known 8px, read back, keep the difference as a bias, then put it back and verify. Three outcomes - spaces agree, constant offset, or anything else. Only the first two enable west anchoring; the third disables it for the session and the west edge resizes like the east one. Degraded, but unrecoverable is off the table.

## Following Figma's own picker

Changing a color in Figma's right rail should move our picker. `documentchange` is the documented signal, but Figma batches it - *"will not call the callback synchronously and will instead batch the updates and send them to the callback periodically"* - so on its own it always trails the drag.

The sandbox can't poll its way out: it's a JS VM with no display, no `setInterval`, no frames. The iframe has `requestAnimationFrame`. So the work got split - the sandbox knows when an edit *started* and can read a paint; the iframe owns the clock. `documentchange` posts a `wake`, the UI then asks once per animation frame until the edit goes quiet. See [[decision-frame-clocked-follow]].

Also needed `lastWritten` echo suppression, or every drag frame would bounce back and fight the picker.

## Rendering

The hexagon's interior field moved to a fragment shader (`perf(hex)`), and handles became fixed-size regardless of panel scale. Brightness got its own track. A handle study went into `docs/prototypes/handles.html` comparing the existing marker against a refined ring.
