14 commits. `feat/figma-plugin` opens, and the Figma plugin exists by the end of the day.

## The constraint that shaped everything

Stated up front by the user: *"when I make changes to the app they need to be immediately applicable to the figma plugin."* So no port, no second picker. `figma/ui/main.tsx` imports the real `src/components/ColorHexagon.tsx` and the plugin build substitutes modules by Vite alias where the panel needs something different. See [[decision-single-source-picker]].

That decision is why the rest of the plugin work reads the way it does - almost every problem since has been solved by finding the right seam in the shared component rather than by writing plugin-specific UI.

## What landed

- The plugin itself: manifest, `code.js` sandbox bridge, `vite.figma.config.ts`, and `inline.mjs` folding the build into a single `ui.html` (relative `<script src>` doesn't resolve inside the plugin iframe).
- Figma theme sync. Figma stamps `figma-dark` on `<html>`; the app's tokens key off `dark`. A MutationObserver mirrors one onto the other.
- Panel chrome removed, icons, stable layout, gutter reclaimed, status moved into the header.
- Content-fit height: the UI measures `.figma-root` with a ResizeObserver and posts it; the sandbox is the only thing that sets height. See [[decision-content-fit-height]].
- Alpha slider and a Fill/Stroke target switch.
- Resize from any edge or corner - which then took two more days to actually behave.

## Bug worth remembering

`localStorage` writes were guarded because they were *suspected* to fail in the plugin iframe. They do fail, and the guard hid it completely - swatches silently never persisted. Not diagnosed until 08-03. See [[decision-clientstorage-swatch-seam]].
