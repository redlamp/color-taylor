The app-not-plugin part came free rather than needing a guard, and it is worth knowing why: `figma.css` flattens `.text-xs`/`.text-sm`/`.text-base` to its own 11px chrome size with unlayered CSS, so a *named* Tailwind step is caught automatically while the old `text-[10px]` literal escaped it.
