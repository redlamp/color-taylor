# 2026-09-03

## The picker gets its demo's ground: `feature/demo`

Prototyped the onboarding demo in an artifact over the last session and settled the layout and
interaction changes it depends on. Ported those to the app today, off `dev`; the demo itself is
next and is specified in [[plan-picker-demo]].

- Recent, Saved and Hex start closed; a `?` button sits between theme and settings, wired to nothing yet.
- RGB and HSB/HSL merged into one flat bank with the plugin's `[RGB][HSB][HSL]` multi-select and
  blend droplet. `BlendIcon` moved from `figma/ui/lite` into `src/components` so both hosts share it.
- Impact highlights: `useImpact` diffs the readouts against a snapshot taken at the press, keyed by
  `data-hold` tags found from a window-level pointerdown. Pure derivation - the first cut set state in
  an effect and the lint refused it, and comparing against the press rather than the previous frame
  turned out simpler anyway. The white keyline tokens moved to `src/utils/highlight.ts`.
- Hexagon: stem and joint halos per channel, channel tooltips at fixed sides of the stem midpoints,
  tip handle a size up with a white ring.
- Playwright's SB-box test toggled the old slider cards closed; it switches the groups off now.

Afternoon: tuned the highlights with Taylor (colour tokens per surface, alpha, bars lighting, the
"never light what you hold" rule written down), the tip back in blue, tooltips fading during a drag
and returning on release. Copied the prototype into `docs/prototypes/picker-demo-prototype.html` and
wrote [[handoff-picker-demo]] for the session that builds the demo.
